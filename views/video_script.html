Part 1: Masterfiles (Full CRUD Demonstration)

0:45 – 3:00
C Create Operation
Screen Action: Click Catalogs on the sidebar → Click + Add Catalog Item → Select Category: Medicine → Name: Amoxicillin 500mg Capsule → Price: 18.50 → Click Save Item. Show it instantly appears in the table with its code (MED-010).
R Read / Retrieve Operation
Screen Action: Type Amoxicillin in the search bar to demonstrate real-time search → Filter by Medicines Only → Change Sort By to Price (Lowest First) → Click the row to open the complete item inspection modal.
U Update Operation
Screen Action: Inside the modal, edit the Unit Price from 18.50 to 22.00 → Click Update Item → Point cursor to the table row updating immediately to ₱22.00.
D Delete / Archive & Restore Operation
Screen Action: Click the item again → Click Send to Archive → Confirm the prompt → Show that the item disappears from active records → Filter by Soft-Deleted Only (or open views/archive.html) → Click Restore Record to demonstrate safe recovery with zero data loss.
Word-for-Word Voiceover: "Every masterfile in our system implements a full CRUD architecture with data integrity guarantees: First, Create: when adding items, unique codes are generated automatically. Second, Read: records are retrieved with multi-column sorting, real-time search, and category filtering. Third, Update: staff can modify prices or patient demographics with instant database updates. Fourth, Delete and Archive: to prevent accidental permanent data loss in a clinical setting, records use soft-deletion. Archived items are removed from active operations but can be restored with a single click."
Additional Masterfile Highlights

Part 2: Transactions (Core Business Logic)

3:00 – 6:30
1. Patient Admission Transaction
Screen Action: Click Admissions → Click + Admit Patient → Select a registered patient → Enter Chief Complaint & Diagnosis → Select an available Bed → Assign an Attending Physician → Click Submit Admission → Automatically navigates to the clinical chart (views/admission_details.html).
Voiceover: "When admitting a patient, the system links the demographic profile, clinical diagnosis, and attending physician, automatically claiming an available bed and marking it occupied hospital-wide."
2. Clinical Charging, Doctor Rounds & Bed Transfers
Screen Action: Click + Log Doctor Round → Submit round fee → Click + Add Charge → Add a Medicine and a Scan → Point to running ledger subtotals updating live → Click Transfer Room / Bed to showcase automated duration and board & lodging calculation.
Voiceover: "During hospitalization, staff log bedside doctor rounds, pharmacy medication issuances, and diagnostic procedures. Each transaction automatically posts to the running patient ledger with exact timestamps and station attributions. If transferred to another ward, the system closes the previous stay, calculates exact room fees, and releases the old bed."
3. Discharge & Final Settlement (The Core Highlight)
Screen Action: Click Discharge & Settle Bill → Check Senior Citizen / PWD Discount (highlight 20% discount + 12% VAT exemption badge) → Enter excess cash to show Customer Change box → Enter partial payment to show Promissory Note Agreement expanding → Select installment terms (e.g. 3 Months) → Confirm Settlement.
Voiceover: "Here is the most critical transaction: Final Discharge Billing. When applying Senior Citizen or PWD benefits, the system computes the 20% discount and enforces the statutory 12% VAT exemption. If the patient tenders excess cash, the system calculates customer change. If the family cannot settle the full amount today, the remainder is formalized into an official Promissory Note installment agreement with guarantor accountability, releasing the patient and freeing the bed."
